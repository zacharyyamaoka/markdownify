# KM80-9 Performance

The peak torque<sup>1</sup> is available for 2 s at most; the continuous rating<sup>2</sup> holds indefinitely.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Rated torque<sup>2</sup> | 9 N·m |
| Peak torque<sup>1</sup> | 18 N·m |
| Rated speed | 230 rpm |
| Efficiency<sup>3</sup> | 86 % |

<sup>1</sup> Measured at 25 °C ambient for 2 s, starting from a cold winding.

<sup>2</sup> Continuous, with the housing on a 300 × 300 mm aluminium plate.

<sup>3</sup> Peak electrical-to-mechanical efficiency at 48 V.
