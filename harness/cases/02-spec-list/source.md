# KM60-6 Key Specifications

## Electrical

- Rated voltage: 24 V
- Voltage range: 18–52 V
- Rated current: 4.6 A
- Peak current: 13.5 A
- Phase resistance: 0.31 Ω
- Torque constant: 0.078 N·m/A

## Mechanical

- Gear ratio: 6:1
- Rated torque: 3 N·m
- Peak torque: 9 N·m
- Rated speed: 330 rpm
- Weight: 315 g
- Outer diameter: 76 mm

## Interfaces

- Communication: CAN 2.0B, 1 Mbit/s
- Encoder: 14-bit absolute, single turn
- Connector: XT30 power, GH1.25 4-pin signal
