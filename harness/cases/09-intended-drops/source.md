# KM80-64 High-Torque Joint

The KM80-64 trades speed for torque: a 64:1 gearbox gives it 48 N·m rated and 120 N·m peak, at a rated speed of 48 rpm.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Rated torque | 48 N·m |
| Peak torque | 120 N·m |
| Rated speed | 48 rpm |
| Weight | 850 g |

Use it for hips and shoulders, where torque matters more than speed.
